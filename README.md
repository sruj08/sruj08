### 📖 About Me

I am a final-year Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering student at **Pune Institute of Computer Technology (PICT), Pune** (CGPA: 8.38), **Co-Founder & Forward Deployed Engineer** at **Novaryn Technologies LLP**, and **Partner** at **MindstriX**.

My engineering focus spans **distributed backend systems**, **production AI/ML pipelines**, and **Web3 smart contracts**. I specialize in taking complex technical challenges from zero to scalable production architecture—whether building resilient Go microservices, deploying LLM RAG pipelines and YOLOv8 computer vision models, or authoring secure Solidity smart contracts on Sepolia and Ethereum testnets.

My work in system architecture and AI governance has been recognized at the highest levels of competitive engineering:
- **Runner-Up at the Pune Agri Hackathon 2026** (Govt. of Maharashtra, INR 15 Lakh Prize Pool out of 2,000+ teams), where our platform was felicitated by the **Hon. Chief Minister of Maharashtra**.
- **1st Rank / Global Winner at TechFiesta '26 International Hackathon** (INR 3.5L+ Prize Pool) out of 700+ teams worldwide.
- **National Winner at VOIS Innovation Marathon 2.0** (Vodafone Intelligent Solutions, INR 2 Lakh Prize Pool) among 630+ teams.
- **2nd Runner-Up at iQOO Hackathon 2026** (Phone-First Computing, INR 50K Prize) for on-device spatial tracking.

**Currently building:** Next-generation AI intelligence and decentralized automation platforms at Novaryn Technologies.
